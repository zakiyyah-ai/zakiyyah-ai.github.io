ZS Sender: personalized WhatsApp messages, sent your way.

A Chrome extension that helps you send personalized WhatsApp messages straight from WhatsApp Web: import a CSV, pick contacts or groups, set your own pace, and everything runs locally in your browser.

What It Does

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

📇

Flexible recipient lists

Build your list from numbers, saved contacts, groups, or ready made lists like Unread and Favourites.

🧩

Personalize with variables

Import a CSV and drop in variables like {name} or {city} so every message reads like it was written by hand.

⏱️

Natural sending pace

Random delays and batching keep sends looking human, with a live report as each message goes out.

👥

Create groups & invite members

Spin up a WhatsApp group and add members straight from your recipient list, in the same panel.

📤

Export your own data

Download saved contacts, unsaved contacts, or group members to CSV whenever you need them.

🔒

Private by design

No servers, no analytics, no tracking. ZS Sender only talks to the WhatsApp Web tab that's already open.

Get Started

Up and running in four steps.

01

Install the extension

Add ZS Sender from the Chrome Web Store and pin it to your toolbar.

02

Open WhatsApp Web

Log in as usual. ZS Sender connects to the tab you already have open.

03

Build your list

Import a CSV, or pick straight from contacts, groups, or lists.

04

Write & send

Add variables, set your pace, and hit start. Watch the live report as it runs.
